Where the agent should take over next, and where it should not

One month of regulated workflow volume across banking, insurance, and government task types. Each task's agent emits a calibrated confidence score; items above the auto approve threshold run end to end, everything else escalates to a human reviewer. The page shows the coverage and exception rate that threshold buys per task, ranks shadow mode tasks by the manual review hours they could remove while staying under a safe exception limit, and sizes the expansion plan.

NOTEIllustrative data modeled on Brain Co.'s public product structure. Not a real customer's or the company's actual data. Built by Vishal Kumar as a candidate work sample, not an official Brain Co. artifact.
